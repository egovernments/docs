eGov Foundation (“eGov”) is the primary contributor to the DIGIT platform and makes the open-source DIGIT platform freely available on GitHub. The DIGIT Partner Program (DPP) is designed to help stakeholders connect with DIGIT, and become part of an ecosystem invested in building citizen service delivery and governance platform - DIGIT. DIGIT enables ease of access to services for citizens, ease of coordination for employees & vendors, ease of decision-making and governance for administrators and ease of policy-making for policymakers & researchers.
